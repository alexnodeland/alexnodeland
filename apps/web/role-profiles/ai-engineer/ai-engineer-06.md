## About the role

A senior engineer on the agent product engineering team of a growth-stage conversational AI company whose platform lets consumer brands deploy customer-service AI agents across voice, chat, email and SMS. The team builds and deploys business-critical agents for large enterprise customers in sectors such as finance, healthcare and hospitality, owning them from first implementation through continuous iteration while working directly with customers.

## Responsibilities

- Design and build AI agents that handle complex customer interactions and retention scenarios better than human agents
- Spot trends across customers that should shape the company's agent-building platform and research priorities
- Trial and evaluate new text and voice models, then roll them out at enterprise scale
- Own agent work end-to-end, digging into complex system problems and building solutions that scale to millions of end users

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years of industry software engineering experience
- Proficiency in Python, TypeScript and asynchronous programming
- Comfort diagnosing system failures deep in complex technology stacks

## Preferred qualifications

- Prior experience working with multi-modal models
