## About the role

A senior AI engineer on the revenue operations AI and data team of an industrial software company. The engineer leads development of production generative AI applications for the company's go-to-market organisation: personalised outreach at scale, seller copilots, and voice and chat interfaces, working with revenue operations, sales leadership, product and engineering.

## Responsibilities

- Build GenAI tools that help sellers identify and reach the right customers faster
- Develop AI copilots that surface CRM insights and sales knowledge in real time
- Launch AI features that improve efficiency and personalisation across the sales funnel
- Work across the stack, from model experimentation through application development to infrastructure
- Collaborate with engineers, data scientists and sales operators in a fast-moving team

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years of industry experience in software engineering, applied machine learning or AI product development
- Track record of building and deploying production AI/ML applications, especially generative AI (LLMs, prompt engineering, embeddings, RAG systems)
- Expert Python skills and experience with GenAI frameworks such as LangChain, the OpenAI SDK and MCP
- Experience building backend systems and APIs and shipping customer-facing features with product and design
- Strong product sense in fast-paced, cross-functional environments

## Preferred qualifications

- AI product work in sales, go-to-market or revenue operations contexts
- Familiarity with enterprise tools such as Salesforce, Gong, Outreach or similar CRM/sales-enablement platforms
- Integrating vector databases, retrieval systems, or streaming voice/chat pipelines
- Leading major technical initiatives or mentoring engineers
- Exposure to cost optimisation, model evaluation, observability and latency management
