## About the role

An agentic AI engineer in the internal IT organisation of a search and data platform company, building autonomous, enterprise-grounded agents that complete business tasks for employees rather than just answering questions. The engineer builds on the company's own search platform (its relevance engine, agent-building and workflow tooling) as the retrieval "memory" for internal agents, and also helps internal business teams get started building on that platform themselves.

## Responsibilities

- Design and implement agentic workflows that use reasoning and tools to run end-to-end business processes
- Ground agents in enterprise knowledge using Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) on the company's search and relevance platform
- Develop and fine-tune LLMs and connect them to internal APIs and third-party SaaS tools so agents can take actions
- Provision and manage cloud environments on AWS, Azure and GCP with Terraform to handle high-concurrency agent workloads
- Set up DevOps practices and CI/CD pipelines for automated deployment, testing and performance tuning of agents
- Apply security and networking controls (VPC configuration, secure API gateways, encryption, IAM) to protect sensitive data and meet compliance needs
- Build AI observability and tracing to monitor agent behaviour, execution latency and token spend, and detect model drift or failure
- Act as subject-matter expert on the company's platform and keep thorough documentation of AI workflows, infrastructure and deployments

## Required qualifications

- Proven delivery of independent GenAI projects involving autonomous task completion or complex workflow automation
- Eagerness to become expert in the company's search relevance engine, advanced RAG patterns and wider platform ecosystem
- Familiarity with LangGraph, LangChain and LangSmith for building and debugging multi-agent systems
- Hands-on DevOps and infrastructure-as-code experience with Terraform, plus containers and orchestration with Docker and Kubernetes
- Strong grasp of secure cloud architecture, private networking, firewalls, VPC peering and identity management (OAuth, SAML, IAM)
- Experience setting up observability (logging, distributed tracing, metrics) for non-deterministic multi-agent workflows
- Awareness of the enterprise agent and workflow-automation platform landscape (e.g. Microsoft Copilot Studio, Salesforce Agentforce, ServiceNow AI Agents)
- Ability to apply trends such as multi-agent orchestration and Model Context Protocol (MCP) to cost-effective enterprise solutions
- Python or TypeScript for backend logic and agent orchestration
- Hands-on experience with LLM providers
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or a related engineering field
- Strong communication and ability to turn business requirements into agent architectures
- Commitment to ethical, responsible AI development
