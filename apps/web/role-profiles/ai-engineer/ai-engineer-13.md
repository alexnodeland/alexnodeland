## About the role

An embedded AI engineer inside the solutions architecture organisation of a large financial-technology company. The team builds tools, agents, automated workflows and custom technical assets that make solutions architects more productive, and success is measured by that org's productivity and adoption of what gets built. There is no dedicated product manager; the engineer finds the highest-leverage problems, scopes them and ships daily, and also builds demo environments and proofs of concept for strategic customer engagements.

## Responsibilities

- Work with solutions architects, their leadership, product and engineering to scope technical work and turn vague business needs into clear deliverables
- Evaluate and integrate AI capabilities (LLMs, agents, workflow automation) only where they give measurable productivity gains
- Design and build internal tools, agents and automated workflows covering technical discovery, solution design, demos, customer engagements, pipeline management and product feedback
- Harden promising tools built by non-engineers into scalable, maintainable, production-grade systems
- Identify recurring friction across workflows and proactively build reusable solutions
- Build custom demo environments, PoC applications and technical assets for strategic customer engagements
- Write documentation of tools, architecture and usage patterns good enough for others to adopt and build on
- Debug, extend and maintain backend systems across multiple codebases and infrastructure

## Required qualifications

- 4+ years as an engineer shipping production systems
- Strong backend fundamentals: debugging failing systems, tracing issues across services, reasoning about data flows
- Has built and deployed AI agents, LLM-powered tooling or workflow automation that goes past basic prompt engineering
- Proficiency in at least two of Ruby, Node.js, Python or Next.js
- Working knowledge of cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP): deployment, monitoring and basic DevOps
- Experience building internal tools, developer platforms or workflow automation
- Ability to work across several codebases and technology stacks at once
- Fluent use of AI-assisted development tools without depending on them to reason or debug
- Scopes and delivers with minimal oversight in fast-moving cross-functional settings
- Clear written and verbal communication with non-technical stakeholders

## Preferred qualifications

- Designing systems that non-engineers can build on or extend (platforms, low-code frameworks, template systems)
- Background in solutions engineering, sales engineering, GTM engineering, or customer-facing product engineering
- Familiarity with the fintech domain
- Integrating with third-party platforms such as Salesforce and Gong
- Tools or systems adopted beyond the immediate team
- Consulting or professional services background blending technical depth with business context
