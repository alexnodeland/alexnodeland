## About the role

A product-oriented senior engineer at a large observability platform company, building AI-assisted workflows into the company's collaborative notebooks product, which customers use for ad-hoc analysis, documentation, incident postmortems and runbooks. The goal is for users to collaborate with each other and with AI agents in the same surface, and to make the resulting knowledge retrievable and reusable across the company's other products. The engineer owns analysis-workflow and context-discovery platforms and works closely with product and design.

## Responsibilities

- Lead design and delivery of AI-powered experiences for the notebooks product and neighbouring surfaces
- Build systems that combine deterministic product capabilities with LLM-powered features to produce trustworthy, explainable results
- Work with the product manager to turn customer problems, adoption signals and roadmap goals into technical decisions and iterations
- Make the company's capabilities usable from third-party AI platforms, agents and chat-based environments
- Provide technical leadership and mentoring and help establish AI engineering best practices
- Build and run reliable backend services in the critical path of customer deployments, including on-call duty

## Required qualifications

- 6+ years of relevant engineering experience
- Experience with Go, Python or a similar systems language
- Hands-on experience shipping production AI-powered, customer-facing products built on LLM technologies
- Strong product mindset linking technical decisions to customer and business outcomes
- Experience building reliable systems that handle model non-determinism through evaluation frameworks, testing strategies and operational rigour

## Preferred qualifications

- None listed separately.
