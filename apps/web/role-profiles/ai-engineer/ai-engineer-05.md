## About the role

An agent engineering role at a growth-stage vertical AI company building agentic software for legal and professional services firms. The engineer builds the systems that make the company's domain-specific agents dependable for professionals: designing environments and actions for agentic work, choosing models, managing context windows, building tools, and developing evals that shorten iteration loops. The company hires for this role at mid, senior and staff levels.

## Responsibilities

- Work with customers and product managers to understand professional workflows, define practical evaluations of what excellent output looks like, and ship agents that complete the work
- Tune agent performance through prompt engineering, model selection, tool design, skill authoring, context window management and eval harness development
- Partner with the model infrastructure team on low-latency agent execution, including caching strategies, parallel tool calls and subagent patterns
- Improve observability and instrumentation to profile agent behaviour, find bottlenecks and guide optimisation
- Track new developments in agentic systems and bring them into the product

## Required qualifications

- 5-8 years of software engineering experience after a BS/MS
- Proficiency in Python and hands-on experience with LLM APIs and agent frameworks
- Experience shipping user-facing products on the backend or full-stack
- Understanding of when and how to use evaluations to drive quality
- Iterative working style: builds proofs of concept, decides quickly and ships early versions
- Adaptable about code and frameworks, and willing to drive adoption of emerging best practices
- Strong interest in building effective domain-specific agents

## Preferred qualifications

- None listed separately.
