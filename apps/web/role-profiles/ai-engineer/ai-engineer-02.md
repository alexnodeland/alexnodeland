## About the role

A senior applied AI engineering role at a frontier model company, embedded with large technology-company customers that are building generative AI features and agentic experiences on the company's model API. The engineer takes use cases from selection and architecture through prototyping, evaluation, production launch and scale, and is judged on shipped production systems and sustained adoption rather than demos. Field learnings are fed back to the company's product and research teams.

## Responsibilities

- Embed with highly technical platform customers as a hands-on thought partner, designing and building new applications on the model API
- Build relationships with customer CTOs and AI product/engineering leaders to get applications deployed at scale
- Write and debug code, build evaluation systems and resolve complex integrations
- Guide trade-offs across model behaviour, reliability, latency, cost, safety, security, governance and operational readiness
- Co-build focused prototypes, pilots and model evaluations with the customer's engineers to test product ideas and steer their architecture and roadmap choices
- Validate upcoming model capabilities in customer environments and pass high-signal feedback to internal product and research teams
- Share knowledge and publish best practices to internal and external repositories to scale the function
- Assess emerging model capabilities, developer tools and implementation patterns and turn them into practical recommendations
- Run on-site workshops and implementation sessions with customer teams

## Required qualifications

- 8+ years of deep hands-on technical experience, plus a desire to work closely with customers (backgrounds include startup founders, software and ML engineers, technical PMs and solutions architects)
- Has personally led complex AI/ML systems at production scale, from vague early discovery all the way to deployment, adoption and measurable business results
- Strong, current knowledge of agent architecture patterns, advanced prompt engineering and context engineering, evaluation frameworks, AI safety and security
- Proficiency in Python, JavaScript, TypeScript or similar, and comfort building production-quality prototypes and reference implementations
- Sound technical judgment about when to go deep personally versus pulling in specialists, and making recommendations with incomplete information
- Ability to juggle multiple projects with broad ownership and frequent context switching in a fast-changing environment
- Humble, empathetic approach to helping others

## Preferred qualifications

- None listed separately.
