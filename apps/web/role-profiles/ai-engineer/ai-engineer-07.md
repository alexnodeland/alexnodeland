## About the role

An AI engineering role at a growth-stage enterprise generative AI company whose end-to-end platform lets large companies build and deploy AI agents grounded in their own data, running on the company's own LLMs. The engineer builds production AI applications and agent features on that platform, turning research into customer-facing solutions, and reports into the head of AI engineering.

## Responsibilities

- Architect, build and deploy high-performance, scalable AI applications to production with solid integration into the company's platform
- Lead development of intelligent agents and AI-powered features, translating research into practical customer solutions
- Partner with research scientists, data scientists and product managers on technical requirements, and deliver the features behind key business needs
- Research and evaluate emerging AI technologies, frameworks and tools
- Promote responsible AI practices including bias detection, explainability and safety alignment

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years building and deploying machine learning or AI systems in production
- Proficiency in Python and experience with AI/ML frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow or JAX
- Solid grounding in machine learning, deep learning architectures and natural language processing, especially large language models
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) and MLOps tooling for managing the AI lifecycle
- Writes clean, efficient, well-tested code for scalable systems
- Strong problem-solving on complex technical challenges
- Collaborates across teams, challenges the status quo and takes ownership of outcomes

- None listed separately.
