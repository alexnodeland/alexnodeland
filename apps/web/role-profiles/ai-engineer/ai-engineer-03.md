## About the role

A product-focused applied AI engineering role at an enterprise foundation-model company, working on the company's secure enterprise AI workspace platform, which connects AI agents to workplace tools. The engineer makes the agents and AI features inside that platform reliable and genuinely useful in real enterprise workflows, working with product engineering, modelling and design. The work spans shipping new product surfaces, building evals and feedback loops, prompt and context experiments, and redesigning workflows so the model is asked to do the right task.

## Responsibilities

- Build AI-powered product experiences that let users create, configure, evaluate and improve automated workflows
- Design and ship new human-AI interaction surfaces such as workflow builders, proactive assistants and review flows
- Build the eval, observability and feedback systems that show whether AI features actually succeed inside real enterprise workflows
- Raise reliability through product design, prompting, context construction, tool-use strategy, instrumentation and feedback loops
- Convert production failures, user feedback and real task data into better behaviour, stronger eval suites, regression tests and signals for the modelling team
- Work with modelling, product, design, customer teams and domain experts to define "good" for specific workflows and ship measurable improvements
- Judge which lever a given workflow needs: tighter product constraints, a better model interface, a stronger eval, or deeper modelling work
- Example problems: natural-language creation of AI automations, evals for multi-step agent workflows, proactive in-product intelligence, interfaces that make agent behaviour inspectable and steerable

## Required qualifications

- Has shipped LLM-powered, agent-powered or AI-assisted product experiences to production and iterated on them from real-world feedback
- Strong software engineer comfortable across backend systems, user-facing product, data, evals and model behaviour
- Thinks in terms of user outcomes and system behaviour rather than model metrics alone
- Strong product taste regarding how interface, workflow and model fit together
- Enjoys debugging messy real-world failures and turning production, user-testing and eval findings into durable fixes
- Owns ambiguous problems end-to-end across system boundaries
