## About the role

A product engineer on the AI engineering group of a corporate card and spend-management company, building the product around an agent that audits customer spend at scale and replaces manual outsourced review work. The agent does the reasoning; this role owns the surrounding product harness — reviewer workflows and UI, data contracts with the company's financial system of record, and feedback loops that improve the agent. The work is mostly backend (system design, data modeling, API design) with a meaningful share of frontend.

## Responsibilities

- Build and ship customer-facing features in production, from sketch through rollout and iteration on customer feedback
- Define and evolve data contracts between the audit agent and the company's financial system of record
- Set up feedback and evaluation loops that surface product signals quickly and close them with product fixes
- Run experiments on customer-facing behaviour, including UI flows, prompt-driven product changes and new review modes, and decide based on data
- Talk directly with customers and reviewers and use what is learned to prioritise the roadmap
- Work with the engineers leading agent design and frontend, pushing back where the seam between agent reasoning and human workflow is wrong

## Required qualifications

- Strong record of shipping customer-facing features end-to-end across backend and frontend
- Product mindset centred on users, workflows and outcomes, with comfort talking to customers
- Bias to action in environments without pre-defined tickets, including shipping net-new products
- Comfort working across team boundaries and challenging decisions outside direct ownership (agent design, UX, data model)
- Strong backend foundation in system design, data modeling and API design, and willingness to build UI when needed

## Preferred qualifications

- Early-stage startup or small-team experience owning a product surface end-to-end
- Building products on LLMs or agentic systems, especially the surrounding harness: evaluation, tracing, feedback loops and human-in-the-loop workflows
- Domain background where review workflows and traceability matter: fintech, compliance, audit or fraud
- Building products on non-deterministic underlying systems
- Being the engineer pulled in to unblock projects spanning backend, frontend and an unowned third system
