## About the role

A senior/staff full-stack engineering role at a growth-stage data analytics company whose web-based notebook and app-authoring platform lets users explore data in natural language or code. The engineer leads development of the product's AI features, partnering with the internal research team on context and prompting and with product engineering on platform integration. As part of the agent platform team, the role owns the scalable infrastructure behind the product's data agents and the experimentation tooling other engineers use to ship new AI features quickly.

## Responsibilities

- Lead the build-out of AI-powered analytics features inside the product
- Collaborate with the research team on context construction and model prompting
- Own the scalable agent infrastructure that powers the product's data agents
- Build experimentation tooling that lets the wider team ship new AI features rapidly
- Integrate AI features with the rest of the platform alongside other product engineering teams
- Lead product initiatives and mentor other engineers

## Required qualifications

- 4+ years of software engineering experience
- Proficiency with React and TypeScript, plus architecture and systems design knowledge
- Experience leading and delivering major product initiatives
- Depth in how the browser and web infrastructure work, to build performant user and internal developer experiences
- High bar for design, correctness and testing
- Strategic thinking that balances velocity with engineering quality
- Interest in the data space and in building tools that empower end users

## Preferred qualifications

- Team stack described in the posting (not framed as a requirement): TypeScript and React frontend with Apollo GraphQL and Redux; TypeScript Express/Apollo GraphQL backend; Postgres, Redis, Kubernetes and Python kernels; CI/CD with Terraform, Helm and AWS
- Curiosity about the wider business, including go-to-market, customer development and hiring
