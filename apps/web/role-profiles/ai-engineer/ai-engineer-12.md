## About the role

A hands-on senior AI engineer on the enterprise technology and AI team of a developer platform company, delivering internal AI solutions with measurable business outcomes. The role puts diagnosis before building: map how work flows across teams and tools, find the real constraint, and confirm AI is the right answer before developing. Initial focus is sales, marketing and customer support workflows, in a remote-first organisation.

## Responsibilities

- Diagnose business problems first by mapping workflows and constraints, and say so when a problem does not need AI
- Own AI initiatives end-to-end, from stakeholder discovery and technical design through deployment and iteration
- Deliver working prototypes in days and ship practical AI solutions focused on measurable value
- Reduce bottlenecks and lead times, measuring success with flow metrics alongside adoption and ROI
- Integrate AI into existing systems and workflows via APIs, orchestration tools and AI platforms, including the company's own agent platform where it fits
- Act as an internal first customer of the company's AI products and feed usage insights back to R&D
- Partner with non-technical stakeholders to align on outcomes before choosing solutions
- Define success metrics and feedback loops that make performance visible
- Evaluate tools, document patterns and build reusable foundations for the team

## Required qualifications

- Strong engineering fundamentals and a preference for the simplest solution that works, with AI layered on solid engineering
- Ability to build, debug and ship production-quality solutions independently, whatever the background
- Strong proficiency in at least one scripting language (Python, JavaScript/TypeScript or similar) and solid understanding of REST APIs, GraphQL and integration patterns
- Prompt engineering as a core discipline: system prompt design, context window management, multi-turn interaction structure, output-quality evaluation and systematic iteration
- Judgment on model selection and cost-performance trade-offs, including small fine-tuned vs large general models and RAG vs larger context windows
- Agentic architecture patterns: tool use, multi-agent orchestration, human-in-the-loop design, guardrails, evaluation frameworks and production reliability patterns
- Practical fluency across the LLM ecosystem, including models from Anthropic, OpenAI and open-source alternatives
- AI safety and risk awareness: guardrails such as input validation, output filtering, access controls, prompt injection defences and data leakage prevention
- Systems thinking and root-cause diagnosis of workflow bottlenecks
- Familiarity with enterprise business systems such as CRM (Salesforce), marketing automation (Marketo), support platforms (Zendesk), integration/orchestration (Workato), AI agent platforms (Relevance AI) and enterprise search (Glean), plus enterprise data models
- Ability to engage stakeholders across varied functions
- Track record of end-to-end ownership of complex initiatives under ambiguity
- Product mindset: scoping MVPs, prioritising and delivering iteratively with attention to adoption and UX

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience with the company's own products
- Consulting, solutions engineering or another customer-facing technical background
- Familiarity with value stream mapping, flow metrics or Theory of Constraints
- Low-code/no-code orchestration tools (n8n, Make, Workato) alongside custom development
- Startup or high-growth company experience
- Mentoring or leading technical projects with junior engineers
