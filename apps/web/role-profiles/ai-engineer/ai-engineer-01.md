## About the role

An engineering role on a frontier model company's applied AI team, acting as a hands-on technical advisor to enterprise software companies that are building the company's model API into their core products. The engineer helps customer product and engineering teams design LLM architectures, build evaluation frameworks and adopt current implementation patterns, carrying a small portfolio of accounts from technical discovery to production deployment alongside sales and product colleagues.

## Responsibilities

- Advise customers on deploying new LLM-powered products and workflows from discovery through launch, coordinating with internal teams to get them to production
- Work with account executives and solutions architects to turn customer business requirements into technical designs
- Shape customer architecture and product direction by building tailored pilots, prototypes and evaluation suites
- Lead technical workshops and code reviews hands-on with engineers on the customer side
- Spot recurring design patterns across customers and feed them back to internal product and engineering teams
- Produce reusable public and internal material on LLM prompting, evals, agentic patterns and system architecture
- Keep current on new LLM capabilities, implementation patterns and AI product development stacks
- Speak at conferences and write blog posts or white papers on applied AI topics

## Required qualifications

- 4+ years in hands-on technical roles (forward deployed engineering, product engineering, software engineering, or technical product management) plus an appetite for customer work; former technical founders welcome
- Production experience with LLMs: advanced prompt engineering, agent development and agent frameworks, evaluation frameworks, transcript analysis, MCP, and deploying at scale
- Strong programming ability in Python or TypeScript, with a history of building production applications
- Comfort with ambiguity and working across domains, favouring simple solutions to complex problems
- Collaborative, cross-organisational working style
- Clear communication of technical concepts to varied stakeholders
- Motivation to build AI systems responsibly
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent combination of education, training and experience

## Preferred qualifications

- None listed separately.
