## About the role

A research engineering position on the audio team of an artificial intelligence company. The engineer is a core contributor to a new generation of open-source audio autoencoders, ASR, TTS, SSL and speech-to-speech models, and is involved across the whole model training process: gathering and processing data, designing novel architectures, and refining training methodology.

## Responsibilities

- Run large-scale audio training jobs
- Optimise the performance of the training stack
- Collect, process and evaluate audio datasets
- Carry out ablations and improvements on architecture and training methodology

## Required qualifications

- Strong research taste and instinct, with the ability to carry a research project from idea through execution to write-up
- Strong implementation and prototyping ability, moving quickly from concept to experiment
- Ability to work well with others in a fast-moving research environment
- Excellent communication and collaboration, working effectively on both research and engineering implementation at scale

## Preferred qualifications

- Expertise and intuition for training audio-domain models, including text-to-speech, ASR, speech-to-speech, speech-emotion-recognition or similar
- Experience training audio autoencoders
- Signal processing knowledge, audio signals especially
- Experience with diffusion models, consistency models or GANs
- Experience training on large multi-node GPU clusters
- A firm grasp of experimental methodology for rigorous ablations and hypothesis testing
- Interest in, and a grasp of, highly parallel data processing pipelines at large scale
- Proficiency with PyTorch and Python
- A track record of contributing to big pre-existing codebases and coming up to speed fast
- Previously published machine learning research at well-regarded venues
- A postgraduate degree in a scientific subject such as computer science, EE/EECS, mathematics, physics or machine learning
