## About the role

A senior backend role on the content protection team of a music licensing company serving video and social content. The team designs and runs the systems that shield those customers from copyright and licensing exposure, detecting and resolving licensing risk across social channels. The engineer works alongside data and machine learning colleagues as well as legal and business operations to produce the precise data needed to enforce rights.

## Responsibilities

- Architect, build and maintain the infrastructure and monitoring alerts behind content protection and rights enforcement services
- Build high-throughput services that continuously fetch, parse and analyse large volumes of usage data from user-generated content platforms to monitor compliance
- Work directly in the data, querying it, spotting weak signals or missed cases, and deciding what to do about them
- Pick up unscoped problems, argue the case for a fix and ship it
- Collaborate with ML engineers on advanced detection models and equip business operations with actionable data

## Required qualifications

- 6+ years in backend software engineering focused on designing and scaling distributed systems
- Proven experience building high-throughput services and data pipelines that handle large data volumes
- Proficiency in modern backend languages such as Python, Go or Java, and in cloud infrastructure on GCP or AWS
- A strong architectural mindset with full ownership of a system from design through deployment
- Comfort with agentic engineering

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience working with user-generated content platform APIs
- Experience with streaming technologies such as Kafka
- Previous collaboration with machine learning teams on data-driven detection tooling
