## About the role

A junior engineering role at a company that makes audio software for professional DJs and musicians. The engineer joins a small agile team working on the company's C++ music production applications, helping both to shape and to implement its core products.

## Responsibilities

- Work in a small agile team on the company's C++ music production applications
- Help shape and implement the core products

## Required qualifications

- Strong programming knowledge, ideally including C++
- Programming experience, ideally gained in a real working environment
- A degree in computer science or a related field
- Strong oral and written communication
- Ability to establish and maintain cooperative working relationships within a team
- Initiative and the ability to work independently while still contributing to the team

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience with or knowledge of multi-threaded or high-performance programming
- Cross-platform application development on Windows and macOS
- Experience with third-party frameworks such as Qt, Boost or Juce
- UI development
- Experience with a web application stack and its programming languages
- Experience with DSP
- Experience with backend database integration, SQL and RESTful web services
- Experience as a musician, producer or DJ
