## About the role

An applied research role on the audio post-training team of a foundation model company working on generative audio. The team is where customer needs meet research, spanning the full modelling cycle from idea to production: designing evaluations that measure new capabilities, building pipelines that improve data quality, and experimenting with fine-tuning and reinforcement learning to shape model behaviour.

## Responsibilities

- Work with product teams to understand and rank customer requests
- Turn vaguely described behavioural problems into concrete research plans
- Experiment across the modelling stack, covering data processing, synthetic data, SFT, RL and evaluations, to deliver high-priority capabilities
- Trace the root cause of failures in production models and work out how to fix them in later iterations
- Judge which features and capabilities are ready to launch publicly

## Required qualifications

- Strong fundamentals in software engineering, machine learning and debugging complex systems, plus the appetite to learn fast
- Experience building large multilingual datasets and assuring their quality
- Experience training and debugging generative models across speech, text or multimodal domains, particularly SFT, RL, synthetic data and both human and automated evaluation
- Enthusiasm for problems rooted in real customer needs rather than benchmark scores alone

## Preferred qualifications

- Native proficiency in additional languages
