## About the role

An engineering role on a newly formed forward deployed team inside the data and insights organisation of a major recorded music and publishing group. The team partners with business stakeholders across the group's labels and global functions to build and deploy automation and AI solutions quickly, collapsing tasks that used to take weeks into workflows that run in minutes. The work is full-stack solution engineering: data pipelining and interoperability, Python back ends, dashboard and Streamlit applications, and the AI tooling offered by the group's data platforms.

## Responsibilities

- Deliver solutions for varied stakeholders, stepping into unfamiliar problem spaces and compressing weeks of work into minutes
- Flex across backend, frontend, data pipelines, AI tooling, Python and React, using AI effectively where depth is lacking while keeping engineering quality high
- Use AI tools to design, build and deploy production solutions that accelerate non-technical stakeholders
- Understand a problem and develop a solution without curated requirements, delivering impact with little starting context
- Communicate in non-technical language with stakeholders from entry level to executive, managing expectations and keeping customers informed
- Work with AI tooling such as Gemini, Cortex Code and Genie Code to turn delivery from weeks into hours
- Explore new technologies and move between different problems and domains day to day
- Contribute to standards and reuse existing tools so solutions stay aligned with the wider data and insights engineering roadmap

## Required qualifications

- At least 3 years of software engineering experience, ideally including exposure to customers
- Experience with Python and with data pipelining such as ETL
- Experience with Snowflake and/or Databricks, either one being sufficient
- Imagination and resourcefulness in finding rapid solutions
- A strong customer focus and bias to action, with motivation to fix pain points
- A passion for learning and an appetite for feedback
- Motivation from the fact that the work affects artists' ability to build careers and get paid

## Preferred qualifications

- Prior use of Genie Code or Cortex Code, notebooks and Streamlit to deliver solutions
