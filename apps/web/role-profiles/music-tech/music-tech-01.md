## About the role

A staff-level machine learning position on the creator-facing side of a large audio streaming service. The team owns the tools and services that let recording artists create, promote and monetise their work, and this role sets machine learning direction for the promotion and monetisation programmes in that area, pairing hands-on modelling with technical leadership across product, research, design and data science partners.

## Responsibilities

- Shape the machine learning engineering strategy for the promotion and monetisation programmes the team runs, converting product aims into scalable technical plans
- Design, build, evaluate, ship and tune production machine learning systems through hands-on coding
- Give technical leadership on complex ML initiatives, helping teams weigh architectural choices against near- and long-term priorities
- Work with user research, design, data science, product management and engineering to create capabilities that strengthen artist-to-listener connections
- Prototype new approaches and harden the promising ones into dependable, scalable services for creator-facing customers
- Drive experimentation, optimisation, testing and tooling that raise the quality and reliability of the team's ML systems
- Explore new music promotion approaches jointly with music technology research and personalisation groups elsewhere in the company
- Raise the engineering bar across the wider organisation through mentorship and knowledge sharing

## Required qualifications

- Deep machine learning experience, with a solid grasp of ML algorithms, modelling approaches, evaluation and experimentation
- Hands-on track record designing and implementing production ML systems at scale in languages such as Python, Java, Scala or similar
- Ability to set technical direction on hard ML problems while staying close to implementation and delivery
- Commitment to reliable software, data-informed development, disciplined experimentation and systems that hold up at scale
- Enjoyment of leading technically complex projects from concept through to production alongside partner teams
- Comfort with ambiguity, trade-off analysis and creating clarity on high-impact work
- Clear communication of technical decisions and risks, with the ability to build alignment among senior technical and cross-functional stakeholders
- A collaborative approach and genuine interest in building products that serve artists and their teams

- None listed separately.
