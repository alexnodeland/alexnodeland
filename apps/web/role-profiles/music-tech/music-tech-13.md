## About the role

A staff-level engineering role in the core engineering function of a global artist development, distribution and publishing company operating in dozens of countries. The engineer designs and builds the systems behind intelligent automation, agentic workflows and large-scale retrieval, turning advanced AI concepts into services that engineering, product, data and business teams across the group depend on. The role is hands-on and deeply technical, with most time spent writing and reviewing code, designing systems and debugging probabilistic rather than deterministic behaviour. The stated stack is Python, Java, GCP including Vertex AI, Kubernetes, Kafka and Kafka Connect, MySQL, MongoDB, Snowflake, Spark, KrakenD and n8n.

## Responsibilities

- Build the enterprise agentic framework: orchestration, retrieval and collaboration between multiple agents; integration of tools and MCP; planning and hand-off; shared state; human-in-the-loop checkpoints; and graceful failure
- Write production-grade, testable code across production services, experimentation harnesses and internal tooling that makes agentic capability easy for other functions to adopt
- Make AI systems measurable and trustworthy through golden datasets; evals both offline and online; regression suites running in CI; guardrails; tracing that spans agent runs of many steps; and telemetry on latency, on quality, on token spend and on failure modes
- Set standards and design for security with platform, architecture and security colleagues, covering system design, internal protocols and API standards
- Raise the level of those around you via code review, design docs, workshops and mentoring
- Champion a cross-cutting technical vision, helping define code and architecture guidelines and weighing complex patterns against their implementation cost

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years in software engineering including a meaningful period at staff, principal or equivalent cross-team level of influence
- Recent experience taking LLM-based systems into production rather than prototypes only
- Strong fundamentals across coding, API design, concurrency, data modelling, testing, CI/CD and containerised deployment
- Practical agentic systems experience: tool calling and orchestration, structured outputs, planning over multiple steps, memory and state, plus the operational reality of long-running workflows that partially fail
- AI workflow knowledge covering retrieval under access control, LLM-as-judge together with its limits, drift detection, model routing, caching, compression, and observability and tracing for systems that are not deterministic
- Influence without authority: aligning stakeholders, writing documents that unblock decisions, mentoring and linking technical choices to product value
- Demonstrated craft sensibility and the ability to teach and embed good practice, with a firm command of testing, software quality and observability, and ease with data-driven steering such as DORA and NFRs
