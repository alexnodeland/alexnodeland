## About the role

A research position in the small research group of a company making an editing tool for video and audio. Rather than general-purpose generative models, the team builds specialised models aimed at particular problems in the editing workflow, and it has shipped such models for a range of audio and video editing tasks. The role centres on multimodal understanding — teaching models to see an edited project much as a human editor would — so that the product's agentic editing features can judge their own output and reason about creative choices, not merely about the structure of a project.

## Responsibilities

- Build vision-language systems letting the agentic editing features reason over a project's visual and audio content
- Design benchmarks and evaluations that make editorial quality measurable while balancing quality against cost and latency
- Build the datasets the work depends on, including synthetic data generation where real examples are scarce
- Train specialised models from scratch or fine-tune existing foundation models, whichever delivers the capability
- Take models from prototype into production alongside the agent and engineering teams
- Identify the research direction that should become the next product feature, an ownership expectation that scales with seniority
- Publish at academic venues if desired, though publication is not required

## Required qualifications

- Proven ability to design and implement deep learning algorithms, evidenced by publications, open-source work or shipped models
- Strong programming skills and deep fluency in PyTorch
- A track record of generating new ML ideas and running and evaluating many experiments quickly once a setup exists
- Strong experimental judgement, testing ideas fast and being candid about which do not work
- Clear written and verbal communication, including when a direction is failing
- A doctorate or master's degree in deep learning or a neighbouring field, or comparable practical experience
- Either lead or first authorship on a paper accepted at a top venue such as CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICML or ICLR, or a key role in shipping a production feature built around deep learning
- For senior and staff candidates, a record of owning research direction and of mentoring or technically leading others

## Preferred qualifications

- Vision-language models and multimodal understanding
- Generative modelling for video, audio or images
- Post-training, fine-tuning and RL on large foundation models
- Building evaluation systems for generative or agentic output where metrics resist clean definition
- Carrying a research idea through to a shipped, production-facing feature
