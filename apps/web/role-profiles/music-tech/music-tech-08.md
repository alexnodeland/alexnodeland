## About the role

A machine learning engineering role at an early-stage company building a wearable voice interface. The engineer develops the system spanning voice, memory and agentic control, and the job is squarely about real-world ML deployment: models must run both in the cloud and on hardware with tight resource budgets, with humans kept in the loop on agentic interactions.

## Responsibilities

- Develop, evaluate and deploy STT/TTS models across cloud and resource-constrained on-device settings
- Optimise inference pipelines for concurrency, reliability and low latency
- Collaborate with the other ML/AI engineers, with infrastructure engineers, designers and the cofounders, on current and future products

## Required qualifications

- 4+ years of machine learning experience
- Experience developing audio or voice models
- Experience shipping ML-based products
- Enthusiasm for human-computer interaction

## Preferred qualifications

- None listed separately.
