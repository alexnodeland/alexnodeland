## About the role

An engineering role at a fast-growing consumer company whose product lets anyone generate music with AI models. The position sits on the internal AI engineering group and is greenfield: building the agent-based tooling and infrastructure that let every function in the business, from engineering through to operations, work faster as the company scales. Two teams are hiring, one focused on developer leverage through agentic tools and one on automating operational areas such as support, legal and finance.

## Responsibilities

- Design and build AI-powered internal systems that help everyone across the company do more with AI
- Own the infrastructure keeping that AI tooling reliable, observable, cost-effective and flexible in production
- Partner with teams across functions to learn their workflows, identify the right problems and prioritise what gets built
- Advocate for good practice in agentic coding and operations and help teams adopt it company-wide

## Required qualifications

- 5-7+ years of full-stack software engineering experience
- A record of designing, building and shipping AI-based tooling well beyond the use of a coding assistant, with fluency in agent loops, memory, MCP and evaluations
- Enthusiasm for learning and sharing agentic coding practices and tools
- Demonstrated delivery of tooling or systems supporting engineering or internal business operations
- Comfort in an early-stage, zero-to-one setting where the problem space is still being defined

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience with agent frameworks such as LangGraph
- Experience with evaluation or observability tooling such as LangSmith or Braintrust
- Having established operational practices for internal systems from scratch
- Familiarity with support tooling, CRM systems or workflow automation platforms
