## About the role

A senior backend role at a music data company supplying monitoring, identification and reporting services to streaming services, publishers and rights organisations. The engineer supports a software team in meeting its objectives, owning technical design and delivery for complex features and system components. The stated stack is Python 3 with FastAPI, a data layer of PostgreSQL via SQLAlchemy, MongoDB via PyMongo and Elasticsearch, CI/CD on Bitbucket Pipelines, containerised deployment with Docker, and infrastructure on AWS.

## Responsibilities

- Own the technical design, implementation and deployment of complex features and system components
- Work with cross-functional teams to define, design and ship new features
- Troubleshoot and debug applications to optimise performance and ensure reliability
- Lead code reviews to maintain code quality and spread knowledge
- Keep up with emerging technologies and industry trends
- Contribute to continuous improvement of the development process
- Turn high-level architectural vision into implementation plans people can act on
- Scope tasks while weighing system and infrastructure upkeep, the cost of migrations and their operational impact
- Align software and systems with the overall technical strategy, making trade-offs in consultation with other engineers
- Mentor junior and mid-level colleagues, sharing expertise and good practice

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years of proven experience as a Python engineer delivering production-ready features autonomously
- Proven work with large datasets and with the handling of big files at scale
- Experience in Python
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail
- Ability to communicate effectively with team members, stakeholders and external partners
- Ability to prioritise and manage several responsibilities at once
- A proactive attitude, adaptability and openness to feedback
- A collaborative approach in a fast-paced environment

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience with asynchronous systems, batch processing or event-triggered workflows
- Experience with web development frameworks such as Django, Flask or FastAPI
