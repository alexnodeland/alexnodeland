## About the role

The first quantitative research hire at a generative music company that builds AI models for artists and fans. The scientist works where research, engineering and product meet, drawing on large proprietary collections of music, metadata and user feedback to push the frontier of music generation. The work covers evaluation and optimisation of frontier music models, ownership of the data roadmap, and the infrastructure needed to make research results usable in production systems.

## Responsibilities

- Design and own the evaluation and optimisation frameworks used for frontier music models
- Probe the internals of the music generation systems, using compute and human raters to map model capability and find weak spots
- Build optimisation loops and feed the findings into pretraining, post-training and inference systems
- Own the data roadmap end to end: frame research questions, find and link new data sources, and run experiments independently
- Carry out work spanning data mining, machine learning, causal inference and survey design to inform product, research and business decisions
- Integrate research output robustly with research and product stacks, including performance-critical paths
- Build large standalone data processing systems and manage the resources behind the data ecosystem
- Establish scientific rigour company-wide by defining metrics, sharing results and deepening shared understanding of models, users and data

## Required qualifications

- Deep quantitative expertise: a PhD in statistics, mathematics, physics or another quantitative discipline, or 5+ years of industry work as a quantitative analyst or data scientist
- Autonomy and ownership, thriving in greenfield research areas, undefined product categories and small flat teams
- Engineering ability to turn ideas into production-ready code and to collaborate inside an active research codebase
- Excellent scientific communication with researchers, engineers, product managers and business partners

## Preferred qualifications

- A fascination with music and the science of sound; background in DSP, MIR, music production, composition or performance
- Familiarity with deep learning frameworks, JAX especially
- Experience with GCP, Apache Beam/DataFlow, Kubernetes, and TensorFlow Data / TFRecord
- Experience designing evaluation frameworks for generative model output in any modality
