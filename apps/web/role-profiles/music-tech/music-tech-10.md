## About the role

A senior engineering role on the audio core team of a music production platform with a catalogue of sounds and samples, an AI stack and DAW plugins. The team owns the desktop application and the company's DAW plugins, including both the audio engine and its user interface, and the engineer is expected to shape the roadmap as well as deliver against it.

## Responsibilities

- Lead the design and implementation of the audio engine behind the company's DAW plugins
- Work with product management and product design to shape the user experience
- Represent the team in technical and commercial discussions
- Propose improvements to maintainability, efficiency or feature depth, win support for them and see them through
- Move between UI and back-end code as the work requires
- Iterate and experiment quickly on new concepts for creative workflows
- Take part in code reviews that shape the codebase and the team's technical discourse
- Act as the resident expert in an area of the codebase and spread that knowledge across engineering
- Align teams on goals and technical trade-offs, authoring RFCs for new features or improvements
- Oversee several concurrent projects tied to organisational goals, aligning stakeholders across functions
- Mentor other engineers and raise the standard applied in code review

## Required qualifications

- 10+ years of software development experience building and maintaining production-quality audio software
- A record of driving complex, ambiguous technical initiatives from problem definition to delivery
- Expert audio engineering knowledge covering signal processing, real-time systems development, multithreading and AI/model solutions
- Expertise in software performance optimisation
- Experience building software with JUCE or another plugin framework
- Production-quality code in C++ and TypeScript, at expert level in C++
- Strong architectural skills, plus excellent communication both in writing and in person
- Strong agentic coding skills, including harness engineering
- A bias towards action, experimentation and quality, comfort working across technical stacks, and enthusiasm for using data in technical decisions

## Preferred qualifications

- Angular UI framework, Rust, Go
- WebAssembly and Emscripten
- iOS or Android development with the native toolchains
- Web Audio
- A musical background as an instrumentalist or vocalist
- Experience with music creation software
- Implementing AI models for inference in a creative field such as audio, image or video
- Training AI models for use in a creative field such as audio, image or video
