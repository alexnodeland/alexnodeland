## About the role

The first dedicated data science hire at an early-stage media and entertainment technology company with a large global audience. The role turns large, messy and hard-to-reach datasets into commercial decisions: spotting emerging cultural trends, predicting which releases travel, and identifying underserved categories. A backend engineer owns the pipelines and infrastructure, so the scientist's time goes on the questions rather than the plumbing.

## Responsibilities

- Find and open up untapped data sources, using creative scraping and acquisition methods where standard APIs stop short
- Analyse trends, audience behaviour and performance across streaming and social platforms to locate growth opportunities
- Prototype and deploy ML models on AWS for virality prediction and content analysis, then verify they hold up in production
- Design and interpret A/B tests, holdouts and spend experiments across releases and campaigns
- Own the dashboards and reports leadership relies on, and investigate what actually moves a metric
- Use LLMs and agentic coding tools to speed up data cleaning, analysis and reporting

## Required qualifications

- A degree in data science, statistics, computer science, physics, mathematics or a comparable quantitative field
- Three or more years of hands-on data science in fast-moving commercial settings
- Expert-level Python with the standard data science and ML frameworks, plus daily SQL
- Demonstrated data acquisition work, scraping and reaching datasets beyond standard APIs
- Statistical rigour: properly designed experiments, the judgement to tell signal from noise, and A/B tests or causal analyses that changed a decision
- Models deployed and kept honest in production over time
- Extensive hands-on use of agentic coding tools and LLMs in data workflows
- Ability to turn data into a proposed next step, with dashboards and visualisations (standard libraries or Tableau) that leadership trusts
- Collaborative working style across engineering, product and business teams
- Comfort in a fast-paced, performance-oriented environment

## Preferred qualifications

- An obsession with music, video or social media
