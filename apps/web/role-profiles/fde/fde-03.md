## About the role

A software engineer with applied AI experience who connects an enterprise AI platform company's agent platform with its customers' engineering teams. The engineer owns the design, build and deployment of LLM-powered agentic workflows, from early prototypes to production agents that integrate models with tools, APIs and data sources, often in regulated sectors such as finance, healthcare and telecommunications. The pace is startup-like, but agents are expected to be reliable, observable, safe and auditable from the start.

## Responsibilities

- Turn high-value, ambiguous enterprise business problems into well-framed agentic workflows with clear success criteria and evaluation methods.
- Lead the building of LLM agents that can plan, reason and take actions over APIs, tools and sensitive enterprise data, meeting enterprise bars for reliability and performance.
- Build and ship features for the company's AI workspace platform across the full lifecycle, from concept to production.
- Scope and shape use cases end to end, moving into whatever technical area the problem needs, including frontend.
- Contribute to shared frameworks and patterns that keep delivery consistent across customers and teams.
- Bring clarity to ambiguous situations, build alignment and raise engineering quality across the organization.
- Travel to work on-site with customers and partners.

## Required qualifications

- Hands-on experience building and deploying production software in Python; writes clean, testable, observable, scalable code.
- Has built and deployed high-performance RAG and agentic applications, including multi-step planning agents using patterns such as ReAct or Plan-and-Execute.
- Deep familiarity with the LLM stack: frontier models, vector databases and orchestration frameworks.
- Proven ability to build robust evaluation frameworks that measure agent accuracy, safety and latency, going beyond trial and error.
- Experience working directly with customers, leading technical discussions with enterprise stakeholders and turning ambiguous needs into concrete technical specs.
- Has owned the full scope of a use case end to end.
- Thrives in fast-paced, ambiguous environments with shifting priorities.

## Preferred qualifications

- Has set architectural standards for AI and agentic systems across distributed teams.
- Willing and able to work in unfamiliar technical areas such as frontend when needed.
- Exposure to regulated or sensitive industries (finance, healthcare, telecoms).
- Experience with enterprise security, compliance or auditability requirements for AI systems.
