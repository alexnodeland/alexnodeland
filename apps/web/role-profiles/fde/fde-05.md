## About the role

A hands-on, customer-facing senior engineer at a data and AI platform company who builds and productionizes solutions to customers' data and AI problems on the company's platform. The engineer owns architecture and design decisions and implements end-to-end systems that span data engineering, AI and application development. The role is billable professional-services delivery: projects are scoped with engagement managers and delivered to specification, and the engineer also contributes reusable accelerators and product feedback.

## Responsibilities

- Lead customer technical projects that deliver production-grade systems: reference architectures, custom applications, data ingestion, and ML/AI model integration.
- Guide strategic customers through large data and AI projects covering end-to-end design, build and deployment; help engagement managers scope the technical work.
- Advise customers on architecture and design, and bootstrap or implement projects that lead to adoption of the company's platform.
- Own architecture decisions so solutions are secure, scalable and aligned with platform best practices.
- Coordinate with the internal technical team, project manager, architect and customer team to deliver the engagement's technical components.
- Work with engineering and support to feed back product issues and speed up resolution of engagement-specific problems.
- Embed with customer teams and work with stakeholders from individual contributors to executives.
- Contribute accelerators, frameworks and best practices that scale across accounts and influence the product roadmap.

## Required qualifications

- 6+ years in data engineering, data platforms and analytics, or software engineering.
- Comfortable coding in Python, Scala or JavaScript/TypeScript and modern frameworks.
- Working knowledge of two or more of AWS, Azure and GCP, with deep expertise in at least one.
- Deep experience with distributed computing on Apache Spark.
- Familiarity with CI/CD for production deployments.
- Working knowledge of MLOps, ML/AI models and AI APIs.
- Has designed and deployed performant end-to-end data architectures and applications combining data pipelines, ML/AI models and user-facing interfaces.
- Experience with technical project delivery: managing scope, timelines and measurable outcomes.
- Documentation and whiteboarding skills.
- Experience with enterprise clients and managing conflicts across a broad set of stakeholders.
- Curiosity and willingness to learn new technologies to complete customer projects.
- Able to travel to customers about 20% of the time.
- A certification on the company's platform.

## Preferred qualifications

- None listed separately.
