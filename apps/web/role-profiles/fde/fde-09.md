## About the role

A hands-on engineer on the deployed engineering team of a growth-stage AI agent tooling company that offers agent frameworks and a platform for observability, evaluation and deployment of agents. The team works with companies building and running AI agents in production, across the full lifecycle from pre-sales technical evaluation to post-deployment advisory, co-designing agent architectures and helping customers operate agents reliably at scale. The role sits between engineering, product and go-to-market, and the posting stresses production systems over demos or research.

## Responsibilities

- Co-architect and co-build production AI agents with customer engineering teams.
- Own the technical win in pre-sales: design proofs of concept, answer deep technical questions and guide evaluations.
- Help customers deploy and operate agent applications such as conversational agents, research agents and multi-step workflows.
- Advise customers after the sale on architecture, best practices and roadmap-level decisions.
- Deliver technical demos, trainings and workshops for developer audiences.
- Feed field learnings back and contribute reusable patterns, cookbooks and example code.

## Required qualifications

- At least 3 years in a related technical position, e.g. software, customer or solutions engineering, or founding/product engineering, preferably at a startup or scale-up.
- Strong Python and JavaScript skills and solid systems fundamentals.
- Has designed agent-based or LLM-powered applications that go beyond simple API calls, including multi-step workflows, orchestration and failure handling.
- Comfortable working directly with customers in POCs, architecture reviews and technical evaluations.
- Explains technical trade-offs clearly and earns trust with developer audiences.
- Owns outcomes rather than just recommendations, with a bias toward action.
- Motivated by operating AI agents in production, not only building demos.

## Preferred qualifications

- Has deployed AI agents in production using common agent frameworks.
- Experience with LLM evaluation, observability or guardrails.
- Experience with cloud environments (AWS, GCP, Azure), containers and basic Kubernetes concepts.
- Has shipped and operated production software under real-world constraints.
