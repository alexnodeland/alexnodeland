## About the role

A customer-facing engineer on the services team of a collaborative workspace software company, leading hands-on technical delivery of its most complex enterprise engagements. The engineer embeds with enterprise customers to design, build and deploy production solutions that integrate the company's workspace deeply into their technical and operational environments. The work includes custom code, production-grade custom agents and AI workflows built with MCP and the company's agent APIs and automation infrastructure, data pipelines, large migrations, and solving problems of scale, permissions and governance.

## Responsibilities

- Own hands-on technical delivery of customer engagements: technical discovery, requirements gathering, solution design and end-to-end implementation.
- Act as a trusted technical advisor on architecture decisions and on expanding use of the product.
- Design, build and operate custom agents, integrations, automations and data pipelines connecting the product to customer systems and workflows.
- Lead large-scale content and data migrations: discovery, scoping, data modeling, transformation logic, validation and post-migration optimization.
- Work with customer engineering, IT, security and business stakeholders to gather requirements, identify constraints, assess feasibility and make architectural decisions.
- Debug hard implementation problems spanning integrations, APIs, permissions, authentication, automation logic, data quality, platform limits and customer-specific setups.
- Run feedback loops with product, customer experience and engineering that turn implementation problems into requirements and tooling improvements.
- Build reusable technical assets: migration tooling, integration patterns, reference implementations, discovery frameworks, standards, scripts and internal delivery tooling.
- Help define how the services team delivers complex implementations at scale (methodologies, scoping artifacts, build patterns, documentation).

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years of hands-on technical work, such as forward deployed or other customer-facing engineering.
- Proficiency in at least one of Java, JavaScript, Node.js, SQL or Python, and comfort writing production-quality code.
- Hands-on experience with APIs and data integration.
- Can lead technical discovery: assess feasibility, identify risks, scope and estimate work, and turn ambiguous requirements into an executable plan.
- Strong debugging and problem-solving across code, APIs, permissions, integrations and customer-specific configuration.
- Strong written and verbal communication with technical and business audiences.
- Record of delivering customer value by turning technical challenges into outcomes at scale.

## Preferred qualifications

- Professional services experience, ideally at a startup or high-growth company.
- Experience deploying autonomous AI agents in complex coding or business workflows.
- Experience designing or implementing AI-powered workflows involving MCP, APIs, LLM applications, prompt engineering, retrieval/RAG systems or workflow orchestration.
- Has built technical frameworks, discovery methodologies or internal tooling from scratch.
- Experience with ETL/ELT, schema mapping, data transformation, validation scripts or tooling for large content migrations.
- History of influencing product roadmaps or architecture decisions alongside product and engineering.
- Experience supporting pre-sales and early engagement phases: discovery, migration scoping, feasibility analysis, effort estimation, and prototypes or custom scripts to validate approaches.
- Track record of successful enterprise customer implementations.
