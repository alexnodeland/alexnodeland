## About the role

An early-career forward deployed software engineer at a data integration and operational decision-making platform company, working directly with customers to understand their hardest problems and to design and implement data-driven solutions on the company's platforms. Projects often begin with an open-ended operational question from the customer. The role is framed around small teams, minimal supervision and end-to-end ownership of high-stakes projects, working with product teams and business-facing deployment colleagues and often on-site with customers.

## Responsibilities

- Work directly with customers to quickly understand their most important problems and design and implement solutions that use their data.
- Own end-to-end execution of high-stakes projects in small, autonomous teams.
- Discuss system architecture with fellow engineers and make technical design decisions.
- Wrangle and integrate very large-scale data.
- Build custom web applications for customer workflows.
- Engage with customer executives and help set strategy for the team.
- Learn new technologies and languages as projects require, sometimes building new ones.

## Required qualifications

- 1+ years of relevant post-college work experience.
- Strong engineering background, ideally in computer science, mathematics, software engineering, physics or data science.
- Strong coder, proficient in a language such as Python, Java, C++ or TypeScript/JavaScript.
- Able and willing to travel up to 25% to client sites.
- Holds, or is able to obtain, a security clearance.
- Fluency in the hiring market's business language.

## Preferred qualifications

- Strongly analytical, keen to tackle technical problems using cloud infrastructure, storage systems, data structures, front-end frameworks and related tools.
- Experience or interest in using large-scale data to solve valuable business problems.
- Works well in mixed teams of technical and non-technical people, and is comfortable with changing objectives and iterating with users.
- Learns continuously, works independently and makes decisions with minimal supervision.
