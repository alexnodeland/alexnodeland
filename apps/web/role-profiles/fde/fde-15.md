## About the role

A software engineer on the core forward deployed engineering org of a finance-automation and spend-management software company, described as an agent-first, high-pace, customer-facing engineering team. The engineer solves the hardest problems between the product and the largest, most complex companies, shaping and shipping the product capabilities needed to grow upmarket. Engineers work directly with customers end to end, from understanding pain points to shaping product decisions to building agents that autonomously extend the product, and specialize within a core product area.

## Responsibilities

- Deliver software end to end for the largest customers: understand user pain points, scope product specs, and build agents that autonomously implement solutions.
- Partner with go-to-market teams (sales, solutions, customer success, account management) to win deals, get customers live and grow the value they get.
- Drive the core product engineering roadmap through an embedded model: build deep domain expertise in one product area and decide when to build a custom solution versus accelerate a broader initiative.

## Required qualifications

- Extensive experience building with and for AI agents.
- Record of shipping high-quality products at scale.
- Comfortable and excited to be customer-facing.
- Wants to work at a fast pace and keep growing in the craft.

## Preferred qualifications

- 3+ years of software engineering experience (stated as preferred rather than required).
