## About the role

A senior engineer at a cloud developer-platform company who acts as a hands-on, consultative build partner to a portfolio of strategic customers building ambitious applications on the company's platform. Rather than embedding full-time with one account, the engineer works across several customers in parallel, shaping architecture, prototyping alongside customer engineers, reviewing code and removing the technical blockers between them and production. Real-world product insights are fed back to product and engineering.

## Responsibilities

- Serve as the hands-on technical build partner for a portfolio of strategic customers, taking each engagement from idea to launch.
- Shape scalable, resilient architectures on the platform and steer customers through key design decisions before they become bottlenecks.
- Develop alongside customer teams: prototype, configure and build reference implementations.
- Review customer code and share best practices for quality, performance and security.
- Diagnose and resolve hard technical problems across the stack.
- Write reference code, samples and technical guidance customers can reuse.
- Support the platform's full breadth when engaged: serverless compute, storage and database products, hosted AI inference and vector search, plus security, networking and observability.
- Take part in customer engineering design reviews, planning sessions and working sessions.
- Surface edge cases, friction and product gaps to product and engineering, and build trusted relationships with customer technical leaders (Staff+ engineers, directors, VPs).

## Required qualifications

- 7+ years of professional software engineering with a record of delivering production systems, not just advising.
- Has owned mission-critical services with real users, including on-call, incident response and high-availability architecture.
- Currently hands-on and shipping, current with modern frameworks, languages and deployment patterns.
- Full-stack depth: frontend performance, backend logic, database optimization and distributed infrastructure.
- Uses AI-augmented development workflows day to day to speed up prototyping and delivery.
- Strong systems design: decomposing complex business requirements into scalable, resilient architectures.
- Deep experience with at least one of AWS, GCP or Azure, including serverless, networking and security primitives.
- High agency in zero-to-one, ambiguous environments.
- Executive presence with VP-level stakeholders, translating engineering trade-offs into business impact.

## Preferred qualifications

- Direct experience building on the company's platform.
- Background at a fast-paced startup or in a customer-facing engineering role working inside a partner's codebase.
- Public technical profile: open-source contributions, technical blogging or conference speaking.
