## About the role

A senior individual contributor on the enterprise engineering team of a growth-stage legal AI startup that offers an AI workspace for law firms and in-house legal teams. The engineer works directly with the largest enterprise customers to understand their needs and build the software that connects the product to their systems and workflows, owning complex projects from an ambiguous request through scoping, implementation and production delivery. The work spans custom integrations and contributions to the core product codebase, alongside product engineers, legal domain engineers and engagement managers.

## Responsibilities

- Learn how enterprise customers' systems, workflows and constraints work, and convert what they need into precise technical requirements.
- Carry delivery across the whole arc: discovery, solution design, build, testing, deployment and troubleshooting.
- Cut through ambiguity between customer and internal stakeholders by questioning assumptions, laying out trade-offs and agreeing on a realistic scope and timeline.
- Build integrations between the product and customer systems, and make changes to the core product; spot where customer-specific work can become reusable capability.
- Run multiple engagements independently: sequence work, track dependencies and flag risks early.
- Diagnose and fix complex issues where the product meets a customer's environment, under time pressure.
- Bring customer insights into product and engineering decisions and help build reusable tooling and engineering practices.

## Required qualifications

- 5-7+ years of software engineering, with recent hands-on ownership of production software and of technical decisions and delivery.
- Strong fundamentals: can independently design, build, test and ship reliable software and work well in an existing product codebase.
- Comfortable leading technical conversations with customers and explaining trade-offs to engineers and non-technical stakeholders.
- Has turned ambiguous, cross-team problems into working solutions and can point to concrete decisions and deliverables.
- Strong product judgment, balancing immediate customer needs against maintainability and broader product value.
- Prioritizes and delivers without detailed direction; sets realistic expectations and surfaces problems early.
- Adapts quickly to unfamiliar systems and changing requirements.

## Preferred qualifications

- Has built B2B enterprise software at a fast-moving company.
- Prior forward deployed or embedded engineering work that involved writing production code.
- Has worked at the boundary of engineering and product, shaping what gets built.
- Knows enterprise integrations, APIs, authentication, and moving data between systems owned by other parties.
- TypeScript experience; it is the main language, though strong engineers from other stacks are considered.
