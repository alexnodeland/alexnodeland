## About the role

A senior engineer at a database and data platform company who works where enterprise customer environments meet a fast-moving internal product. The engineer partners directly with customers to design, build, troubleshoot and improve production solutions and is ultimately accountable for getting customers to production. The role suits engineers who want end-to-end ownership of technical outcomes: understanding what the customer is building, shipping the integration, and feeding learnings back into the roadmap.

## Responsibilities

- Act as primary technical owner of customer engagements from discovery through production rollout.
- Learn each customer's architecture, constraints and definition of success, and drive toward it; manage expectations and communicate risk clearly.
- Write the connectors, data pipelines and support tooling that let the platform run in actual enterprise environments.
- Write production-quality code, troubleshoot and ship fixes directly within active workstreams.
- Work within customer environments with differing stacks, infrastructure and integration constraints.
- Capture precise product feedback (logs, reproduction steps, proposed fixes) and identify product gaps and recurring patterns.
- Document technical decisions and trade-offs so product and engineering teams can extend the work.
- Partner with product and engineering to turn field patterns into reusable product capabilities, contributing to technical design and implementation.
- Coordinate independently across internal teams and customer stakeholders, and communicate with technical, business and executive audiences.

## Required qualifications

- 5 to 8 years of engineering experience.
- Hands-on production engineering: writing code, debugging failures and implementing fixes directly.
- Engineering experience in AI and agentic development.
- Experience developing and deploying software in enterprise environments.
- Has shipped production solutions at scale that customers depended on.
- Experience building integrations across existing systems and infrastructure.
- Strong troubleshooting and code review skills, including root-cause analysis.
- Willing to travel up to 30% for on-site engagements, technical reviews and presentations.

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience with LLMs, agent frameworks or deploying AI systems in production.
- Prior forward deployed engineering or similar experience.
