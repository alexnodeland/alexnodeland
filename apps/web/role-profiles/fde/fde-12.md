## About the role

A deeply technical, hands-on engineer at a growth-stage AI startup whose agentic platform lets people build software applications from natural language. The engineer works with very large organizations to turn ambitious ideas into production applications on the company's platform, understanding technical and business needs, architecting solutions, and building the integrations and applications needed to deploy inside complex enterprise environments. The role covers taking applications from prototype to production while working through real-world infrastructure, data, security and organizational constraints.

## Responsibilities

- Work embedded with key enterprise accounts to design and ship high-value apps and AI workflows on the platform, from first idea to production.
- Architect secure, scalable connections between the platform and customers' identity providers, APIs, data, infrastructure and existing systems.
- Handle integrations with customer SSO, data warehouses, internal APIs and SaaS tools.
- Act as technical owner of complex enterprise implementations: identify blockers, debug issues and drive projects to production adoption.
- Translate field insights, constraints and recurring needs into actionable feedback for product and engineering.
- Collaborate with product, design and engineering; ship quickly and iterate on customer feedback and usage data.
- Turn field patterns into templates, playbooks and product requirements, and ship the best patterns back into the platform.

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years of software engineering building and deploying production software.
- Strong programming skills and the ability to get up to speed quickly on unfamiliar codebases, APIs and environments.
- Hands-on production experience with LLM applications, specifically retrieval, tool calls and evals.
- Has designed and owned complex technical systems from architecture through production.
- Operates effectively in ambiguity and drives technical projects to completion independently.
- Strong technical communication and experience working directly with customers, partners or cross-functional stakeholders.
- Turns business requirements and customer problems into pragmatic technical solutions.

## Preferred qualifications

- Prior work as an FDE, solutions architect, sales engineer, consultant or in another deeply technical customer-facing job.
- Experience building and deploying AI-powered applications, agents or developer tools.
- Familiar with current cloud infrastructure, deploying applications, databases, APIs, authentication and common enterprise integration patterns.
- Has fitted software into complex enterprise estates, dealing with existing developer infrastructure, internal data sources, APIs, and identity and access management.
