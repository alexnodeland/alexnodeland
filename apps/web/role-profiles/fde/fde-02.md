## About the role

A founding member of an AI model company's forward deployed engineering function, sitting within its applied AI team. The engineer embeds with strategic enterprise customers to ship production AI applications built on the company's models, both accelerating adoption of existing products and creating new applications. The role blends engineering depth, knowledge of frontier AI applications and customer-facing skills, and expects the engineer to operate autonomously in ambiguous customer environments while working with post-sales, product and engineering teams.

## Responsibilities

- Build production applications on the company's models from inside customer systems, making sure they meet customer requirements.
- Deliver technical artifacts for production workflows such as MCP servers, sub-agents and agent skills.
- Provide high-touch deployment support for the company's products in enterprise environments.
- Identify and codify repeatable deployment patterns and feed insights back to product and engineering.
- Stay current on LLM capabilities, implementation patterns and AI product development stacks.
- Build long-term customer relationships and spot new opportunities for AI deployment over the course of an engagement.
- Travel frequently to customer sites to build in person.

## Required qualifications

- 4+ years working in customer-facing technical positions (for example as an FDE, or a software engineer who has also done consulting or product work); ex-technical founders are encouraged.
- Has run LLM systems in production, covering agent development, advanced prompt engineering, evaluation frameworks and at-scale deployment.
- Strong programming ability in Python, ideally plus another language such as TypeScript or Java, with a record of shipping production applications.
- High agency and comfort navigating ambiguity in complex organizations.
- Collaborative, low-ego approach to cross-organizational work.
- Strong communication skills for customer discovery and for explaining technical concepts to varied stakeholders.
- Interest in building AI systems responsibly.

## Preferred qualifications

- Background in financial services, healthcare/life sciences or another enterprise vertical.
- Experience with enterprise IT systems and/or AI deployment patterns.
