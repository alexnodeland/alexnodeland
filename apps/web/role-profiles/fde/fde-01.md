## About the role

An engineer on a frontier-model company's forward deployed team who leads complex, end-to-end deployments of the company's models inside its most strategic customers. The role covers discovery, technical scoping, system design, the build itself and the production rollout, working directly with customer engineering and domain teams. Success is measured by production adoption, measurable impact on customer workflows, and eval-driven feedback that shapes the company's product and model roadmaps. The team sits between customer delivery and core platform development, and works with product, research, partnerships, security/GRC and go-to-market teams.

## Responsibilities

- Own technical delivery across several concurrent deployments, from the first prototype through to stable production.
- Build full-stack systems that create customer value and improve how the company learns from deployments.
- Embed with customer teams, understand their needs, and drive adoption of what gets built.
- Scope work, sequence delivery, and clear blockers early.
- Trade off scope, speed and quality, and re-plan to protect delivery.
- Write code directly whenever progress or clarity depends on it.
- Turn working patterns into reusable tools, playbooks and building blocks for other teams.
- Feed field observations back to research and product about where the models succeed and where they fall short.
- Keep cross-functional teams moving through clear communication and follow-through.

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years of engineering or technical deployment experience, including customer-facing work.
- Has scoped and delivered complex systems in fast-moving or ambiguous settings.
- Writes and reviews production-grade frontend and backend code in Python, JavaScript or a comparable stack.
- Has built or deployed systems powered by LLMs or generative models, and understands how model behavior affects the product experience.
- Makes fast, sound decisions under pressure and simplifies complexity.
- Communicates clearly with engineers, product teams and customer stakeholders.
- Spots risks early and adapts without losing pace; stays calm and exercises judgment in high-stakes situations.

## Preferred qualifications

- None listed separately.
