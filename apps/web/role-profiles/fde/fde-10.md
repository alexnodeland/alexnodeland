## About the role

A technical, customer-facing delivery engineer at a growth-stage conversational AI startup whose platform lets enterprises deploy customer-service agents across voice, chat, email and SMS. The role owns end-to-end execution of AI agent builds for strategic customers: writing and configuring key components, validating integrations, evaluating behavior and making sure agents perform reliably at scale. It sits in a newly formed specialist deployment engineering team and works closely with agent product managers, customer success, engineering and go-to-market.

## Responsibilities

- Take enterprise agent builds from start to finish: scoping and system design, then implementation, evaluation and go-live.
- Write the core decision logic that controls how agents behave, tuning for correctness, quality and reliability even though model outputs are non-deterministic.
- Build and test multi-layer guardrails and supervisory checks so agents behave safely, compliantly and predictably.
- Architect, build and test integrations with customer systems (data pipelines, CRMs, ticketing systems), including the tools, APIs and workflows needed for reliable deployment.
- Define success criteria and system requirements with senior customer technical stakeholders and drive delivery against timelines.
- Root-cause both probabilistic (model) failures and conventional technical failures by digging through execution traces, error logs and model behavior.
- Build evaluation and regression-testing frameworks that compare agent behavior with ground truth and detect performance drift.
- Write clear internal documentation from customer needs and run tight feedback loops with engineering on platform improvements.

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years in a technical customer-facing role such as solutions engineering, forward deployed engineering, technical consulting, implementation engineering or technical product management.
- Strong technical foundation: comfortable writing code, working with APIs, and building and validating integrations end to end.
- Has delivered production-grade customer solutions requiring structured execution, testing/validation and iteration.
- Communicates clearly with senior technical stakeholders and turns requirements into implementation plans.
- Comfortable in fast-moving, ambiguous environments, shaping solutions as well as implementing them.

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience building with or around LLMs and AI agents: prompting, evaluation, guardrails, tooling and workflow design.
- Experience with enterprise SaaS integrations (ticketing, CRM, data pipelines) and their security and compliance considerations.
- A computer science, engineering or math degree, or equivalent experience.
- Strong product instinct: writes crisp PRDs, defines success metrics and feeds customer insight into the roadmap.
