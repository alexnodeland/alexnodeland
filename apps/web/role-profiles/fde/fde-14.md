## About the role

A founding member of a design tooling company's new forward deployed engineering team, positioned as a senior-to-staff engineering role within product and engineering rather than sales or services. The engineer embeds with strategic customers, getting into their code, developer environments, design systems and day-to-day workflows so that the company's products, including its AI and design-to-code tooling, succeed within their technical constraints. Problems are solved hands-on, often by writing product code, and then turned into reusable paths for future customers rather than permanent ownership of a customer's codebase. The role can be held from a company hub or remotely within the country.

## Responsibilities

- Make an enterprise design system ready for AI agents so that AI tools generate faithful, on-system code: fix design tokens, naming and guidelines, connect design components to code, and run evals.
- Get the company's newest AI and code-generation products working in a customer's real environment by writing code, configuration, agent skills and guideline files against both the customer's systems and the company's.
- Run technical discovery on a customer's dev environment, toolchain and constraints, and scope the fastest credible path to value while setting up long-term success.
- Debug hard, unfamiliar integration problems such as git workflows, CI, dev-environment setup and non-standard stacks.
- Set up LLM-based workflows, including MCP configuration, agent skills and customer-specific prompt engineering.
- Turn one-off fixes into reusable playbooks and product feedback so solved patterns become self-serve or shipped features.
- Bring friction learnings back to product and engineering, influence what gets built, and often build it.

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years delivering production software end to end, including a substantial stretch working embedded with customers or partner teams.
- Strong communicator and technical partner in front of customers, able to win the trust of senior engineers.
- Strong engineering fundamentals and quick onboarding into unfamiliar codebases, languages and environments.
- Hands-on experience with design systems and design-to-code workflows: component libraries, tokens, documentation and the tooling linking design artifacts to production code.
- Proficiency in TypeScript/JavaScript or Python, with demonstrated fluency in agentic coding tools (e.g. MCP, agent orchestration).
- Hands-on experience integrating APIs and SDKs, troubleshooting toolchains and CI environments, and handling enterprise constraints like access controls, SSO, data residency, security reviews and compliance frameworks.
- Comfortable owning ambiguous problems end to end and turning field learning into reusable product improvements.

## Preferred qualifications

- Background in forward deployed engineering, solutions engineering, professional services, developer experience or a similar customer-facing technical role.
- Familiarity with developer tools, design-to-code workflows or AI/LLM products, including Storybook, Style Dictionary or comparable technologies.
- Has partnered with sales, customer experience or solutions architecture teams on complex enterprise engagements.
