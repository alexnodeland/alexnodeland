guitar this time, and out on long island rather than up in harlem.

all shook up is a jukebox musical, two dozen elvis presley songs hung on a book set in 1955. ck productions staged it at town hall west in islip, in association with the town, and it ran from 25 june to 10 july 2016. playing this material is a different job from playing a revue. everyone in the room knows the parts before you play them, so there is no version of approximately right.

that was the last of them, five years after the first.
