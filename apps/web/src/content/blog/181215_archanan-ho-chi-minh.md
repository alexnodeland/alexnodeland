december 2018, [five months after the investor day](/timeline/180724_ef-investor-day/), we took a second office, in ho chi minh city. it moved around a bit before settling at [the hive in thảo điền](https://thehive.com.vn/).

the team there grew to five engineers, and i did not manage them from singapore: every month i flew down and worked from the office with them, until covid closed the borders.
