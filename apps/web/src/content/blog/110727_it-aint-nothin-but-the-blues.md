bass guitar and double bass, five weeks at aaron davis hall on the city college campus at 135th and convent.

new haarlem arts theatre was new that summer. city college's department of theatre and speech had just launched it, and this was part of the opening season, directed by alfred preisser. the show is a revue rather than a book musical, which changes the job in the pit. there is no plot to underscore and nobody to follow into a scene. the songs are the evening, and the band is playing the thing itself rather than accompanying it.

the run went from 27 july to 21 august 2011, thursdays through saturdays with weekend matinees. the new york times reviewed it.
