new haarlem arts theatre staged [sweet charity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweet_Charity) at aaron davis hall in the summer of 2012, and i played bass guitar and double bass in the pit. the band was brett pontecorvo's, and brett is how i got the call.

[julio agustin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julio_Agustin) directed it as a latino re-conception rather than a straight revival. the characters moved toward the cast, so charity became caridad, while cy coleman's music, dorothy fields's lyrics and neil simon's book were left as written. that leaves the band playing the score exactly as printed underneath a show that has shifted around it. edlyn gonzález played caridad, and agustin, who had spent years on broadway in fosse, chicago and steel pier before he moved to [directing](https://www.julioagustin.com/director), took an audelco nomination for this one.

the run went from 26 july to 19 august. the reviews were lukewarm on the concept and warm on the dancing, and the new york times, going through lainie munro's choreography, credited "sure-footed accompaniment from brett pontecorvo's small band". that is the closest i have come to being reviewed. the times also filmed an excerpt, which is the only footage i know of.
