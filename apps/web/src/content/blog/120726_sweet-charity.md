bass again, back at aaron davis hall the following summer.

this one was a re-conception rather than a revival. julio agustin directed, the cast was latino, and charity became caridad, but cy coleman's music, dorothy fields's lyrics and neil simon's book stayed where they were. leaving the score alone is the harder version of that idea. rewriting it would let you settle every question in advance, and instead the songs have to carry a world they were not written for. edlyn gonzález played caridad.

the run went from 26 july to 19 august 2012. the times reviewed it and filmed an excerpt, which is the closest thing to a recording of any of this that i know of.
