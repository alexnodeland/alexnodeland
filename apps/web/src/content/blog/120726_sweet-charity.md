new haarlem arts theatre staged [sweet charity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweet_Charity) at [aaron davis hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Davis_Hall) in the summer of 2012, and i played bass guitar and double bass in the pit.

[julio agustin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julio_Agustin) directed it as a latino re-conception rather than a straight revival. the characters moved toward the cast, so charity became caridad, while cy coleman's music, dorothy fields's lyrics and neil simon's book were left as written. that leaves the band playing the score exactly as printed underneath a show that has shifted around it, and the songs have to hold up somewhere nobody wrote them for. edlyn gonzález played caridad. agustin had spent years on broadway in fosse, chicago and steel pier before he moved to [directing](https://www.julioagustin.com/director), and took an audelco nomination for this one.

the run went from 26 july to 19 august. the new york times reviewed it and filmed an excerpt, which is the only footage i know of.
